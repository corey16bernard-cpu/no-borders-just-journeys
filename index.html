Schengen in 20 seconds

One area, 29 countries, zero hassle

The Schengen area is the part of Europe where countries dropped routine passport checks between each other. For you, that means one trip can be ten countries long.

29

countries in the area: 25 EU members plus Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway and Switzerland.

0

routine passport checks at internal borders. Trains, buses and ferries roll straight through.

3.5M

people cross an internal border every day, for work, study or the weekend.

1985

the year it started, with a deal signed in Schengen, a village in Luxembourg.

Heads up: Ireland and Cyprus are in the EU but not in Schengen, and neither is the UK. Expect checks there.
